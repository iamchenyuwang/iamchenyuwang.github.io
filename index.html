01

Research

I study how AI can become a capable engineering collaborator—and how the computing stack should change when intelligence is composed from many specialized models and tools.

A

Agents for system design

Foundation models and simulator-in-the-loop agents that reason over architecture, hardware/software co-design, and system optimization.

B

Compositional intelligence

Methods and systems that coordinate specialized small models to improve reasoning quality without relying on ever-larger monolithic models.

C

Efficient AI systems

Algorithm–architecture co-design, processing-in-memory, and efficient inference for emerging neural workloads.
